Why the Chase Sapphire Preferred is my Next Credit Card Choice

Over the past few months, I have been researching and comparing several different credit cards. As of the time of writing this post, I only have a line of credit with Discover. Although my Discover student card has been a solid starter card, I am ready to jump into mid-tier cards because of my job. After careful consideration, I've landed on the Sapphire Preferred from Chase. Here's why.

Outstanding Travel Rewards

The Chase Sapphire Preferred offers impressive travel rewards that align perfectly with my lifestyle. With 2x points on travel and dining at restaurants, I can quickly accumulate points on my everyday spending. Working in consulting, I anticipate eating out and travelling quite a bit, so I plan on getting some use out of this aspect of the card. Furthermore, these points can be redeemed through the Chase Ultimate Rewards portal for 25% more value, making my travel rewards stretch further.

Attractive Sign-Up Bonus

The sign-up bonus for the Chase Sapphire Preferred is a major draw. Currently, new cardholders can earn 60,000 bonus points after spending $4,000 on purchases in the first three months from account opening. I anticipate I will be able to hit this spend target. I have a few big ticket items I have to pay for here shortly. Namely, my student loan repayment grace period is about to end. Additionally, I have some repairs to make to my car and I would also like to finish my home office. I anticipate with these needs I will be able to hit the $4,000 spend within the first three months. Like the travel points, when redeemed through Chase Ultimate Rewards, these points are worth $750 towards travel. This bonus provides a fantastic head start on earning rewards and offsetting travel costs.

Annual Fee

Unlike some premium cards, the Chase Sapphire Preferred has a more manageable annual fee of $95. This lower fee makes it an accessible option for those who want to enjoy premium rewards without the higher cost associated with some other cards.

Travel and Purchase Protection

The Chase Sapphire Preferred includes valuable travel and purchase protections. These benefits include trip cancellation/interruption insurance, auto rental collision damage waiver, and purchase protection. These features offer peace of mind and financial protection for both travel and everyday purchases. While this wasn't a big selling point for me, it's nice to know that I have access to this benefit if I ever need it.

1:1 Point Transfer to Travel Partners

One of the standout features of the Chase Sapphire Preferred is the ability to transfer points on a 1:1 basis to several leading airline and hotel loyalty programs, such as United MileagePlus, Southwest Rapid Rewards, Marriott Bonvoy, and World of Hyatt. This flexibility allows me to maximize the value of my points and tailor my rewards to my specific travel needs.

No Foreign Transaction Fees

For an international traveler, having no foreign transaction fees is a significant benefit. The Chase Sapphire Preferred allows me to use my card abroad without incurring extra charges, making it an excellent choice for international purchases.

Dining and Entertainment Benefits

The Chase Sapphire Preferred also offers rewards on dining and entertainment. With 2x points on dining at restaurants, including takeout and delivery services, I can earn rewards while enjoying meals and experiences. This is the benefit I see myself gaining the most utility from. The card is a versatile option for both everyday spending and special occasions.

Access to Ultimate Rewards Experiences

As a Chase Sapphire Preferred cardholder, I also gain access to exclusive Ultimate Rewards experiences. These can include special events, unique experiences, and other perks that enhance my lifestyle and add extra value to my card membership.

Other Determining Factors

I would say it came down to the Chase Sapphire Preferred and the American Express Gold card for my top two choices to enter into the mid-tier/premium travel card game. Honestly, I could have seen myself going with either option and will most likely end up with both down the road. The main reason I wanted to get the Chase Sapphire Preferred card first was because I already have a debit, checking, and investment account with Chase, so it seemed natural to extend a line of credit with them as well. Eventually, I would also like to upgrade the Sapphire Preferred to the Sapphire Reserve, Chase's most luxorious card. That will have to wait awhile, however, since the annual fee for the Sapphire Reserve is almost $1000 per year and really only makes sense for big spenders.

In conclusion, I am extremely happy with everthing the Chase Sapphire Preferred card has to offer. I can see myself unlocking some nice rewards given how I plan to use the card. As I touched on earlier, I think the Sapphire Preferred and the American Express Gold Card are the clear leaders in this category of credit cards. If you are thinking of one of these two, I think they are both great options. It just happened to be the case that the Sapphire Preferred made the most sense for me given my situation.
